# OrderHub AI Policy

AI tools may assist with this independently maintained local project. A human remains responsible
for deciding what work to accept and for reviewing changes that can affect live trading.

## Human responsibility

- Review and understand generated code, documentation, tests, and claims before using them.
- Validate behavior with relevant local checks. Describe limitations and unverified assumptions.
- Authorize public repository interactions, deployment, publication, and trading operations.
- Do not use AI to disguise third-party code or make legal representations about rights to submit
  material. Preserve original copyright, license, and source attribution.

AI tools cannot accept a contributor agreement or certify legal rights on a person's behalf.
The historical upstream [CLA.md](CLA.md) is retained for provenance and is not a local
contribution requirement.

## Quality and communication

Changes to order admission, risk, execution, persistence, recovery, or reconciliation need
appropriate tests and a clear explanation of what was verified. Automated output requires the
same scrutiny as hand-written work. Reports and review text should state concrete behavior and
evidence without suggesting endorsement by the upstream NautilusTrader project.

AI assistance need not be disclosed unless an applicable legal or contractual obligation requires
it. A disclosure, if chosen, should be accurate and should not imply that an AI tool owns the work.
